📸 Photo Showcase

Pro photos and slideshows from your cuts

Add your shop photos, crop each one by hand, and give them a clean, professional look for Instagram, theCut, TikTok or any portfolio. Mark every shot Before or After and put each client's shots together, then play everything as a slideshow with smooth barber-style transitions and download the photos, collages and video.

📁 Project: New project

Saves by itself as you work

Make one project per client (or per day). Everything — photos, before & after, effects, text and music — saves on this device by itself, so you can leave one and come back to it later. Tap a project to open it.

💾 Backup & move to another phone or computer

Make a backup file and keep it in iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Files or your email. On any device, open this page and tap Open backup file to get your projects back.
