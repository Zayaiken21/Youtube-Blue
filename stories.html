    Which formats work?
    • ALEX: Ugh, the Wi-Fi is down! — a name, then a colon (times like 00:05 in front are fine)
    • A name in CAPITALS on its own line, with their words right under it (screenplay style)
    • MOM & DAD: Happy birthday! or ALL: Deal! — said together (a group line)
    • NARRATOR: goes to the Narrator. If your main character tells the story, write ALEX (narrating): … or NARRATOR (Alex): …, or pick them under Narration is read by
    • A list of how each character sounds (CHARACTERS / Voice tones, then Customer: Confused, then outraged.) becomes their role, not spoken lines
    • Only labeled lines are spoken: text with no name in front is left out unless you choose otherwise · Scene 2: / INT. KITCHEN start a scene · [SFX: …] and (laughing) become directions and reaction tags
    • A Characters: list with Name – role fills in each character's role
