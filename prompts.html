🧠 Prompts

Prompts for your AI

Copy a ready-made prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Every prompt makes your AI answer in the YouTube Blue Script Format, so you can paste the result straight into Story Studio → 📄 Import — characters, roles, scenes and lines land in the right place, and notes never become spoken lines.

The YouTube Blue Script Format

What the prompts ask your AI to write. You can also type a script this way yourself — only the lines inside === SCRIPT === are ever spoken.

TITLE: The Great Car Packing Disaster
=== CHARACTERS ===
JAYDEN | Main character, 9, narrates his own story | Fast, bright kid voice
AUNT NICOLE | Cheerfully unreasonable about luggage | Calm, warm
=== SCRIPT ===
JAYDEN (narrating): I said I could pack the car in ten minutes.
AUNT NICOLE: Just three small bags. And the inflatable flamingo.
SCENE: The driveway
JAYDEN + AUNT NICOLE: [gasp] We forgot Grandma!
ALL: Start the car!
=== END ===
(anything after END — storyboard, image prompts, hashtags — is ignored)
  • NAME | role | voice under CHARACTERS becomes each character's role and voice notes — never a spoken line.
  • Under SCRIPT, every line is NAME: spoken words. Lines without a name are left out.
  • A + B: or ALL: say a line together · NAME (narrating): when the main character tells the story · NARRATOR: only for a real narrator.
  • SCENE: starts a scene (not spoken) · [laugh] [sigh] [gasp] and other reaction tags go inside the line.
