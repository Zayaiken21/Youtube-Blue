🎨Channel Design

Your brand kit and ready-to-upload art.

  • Banner at 2560×1440 with device safe-area guides
  • Profile picture (800×800) with circle preview
  • Thumbnail maker (1280×720) with photo upload
  • About-section writer, keywords and launch checklist

📊Analytics

Log or import your numbers and see what works.

  • Views, watch hours, CTR and engagement totals
  • Views-over-time and top-video charts
  • Shorts vs long-form and best upload day
  • Goal tracker, CSV import (YouTube Studio exports) and export

🗓️Upload Scheduler

Find your niche's best times and plan the week.

  • 17 niche benchmarks, plus your own custom niches
  • 7-day audience heatmap with the best windows starred
  • Tap a slot to add a Short or long-form upload
  • 3-a-day notification cap warnings and an optimization score

🎞️Comic to Video

Turn comic pages into a YouTube Short.

  • Panels found by their white borders, in story order
  • Reorder, remove or draw panels by hand
  • Zoom, pan and smooth transitions in 9:16
  • Add your Voice Studio story and export the video

✍️Story Studio

Script templates and a cast you build with “+”.

  • Templates: 3-act, Shorts, Bible story, animal facts, funny clips, explainer
  • One-voice or multi-character narration modes
  • Runtime estimate, per-voice recording sheets
  • Teleprompter with auto-scroll and mirror mode

🎙️Voice Studio

Turn scripts into narration on your Chatterbox server.

  • Built-in voices or clone your own from a short clip
  • Import any story — all lines or one character at a time
  • Expression tags like [laugh] and [sigh]
  • Live progress, cancel, play and one-tap download

  1. Tap Install app above, or open the browser ⋮ menu.
  2. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
