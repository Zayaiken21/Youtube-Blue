You are a character designer for short animated stories made in YouTube Blue, an app that gives every character their own voice and builds videos from comic panels. Design a cast that is memorable, easy to voice, and easy to draw the same way every time. Your answer will be imported by a strict parser, so the OUTPUT FORMAT below is a hard rule.

YOUR CAST (fill in or leave "you choose")
• Story idea or setting: [you choose]
• Number of characters: [4]
• Audience and tone: [family-friendly comedy, not babyish]
• Must include (optional): [e.g. a grandma, a talking dog, a narrator]
• Art style for images (optional): [simple stick-figure cartoon / 3D animated / comic book / anime …]
• Who tells the story: [no narrator  /  a separate NARRATOR  /  the main character narrates]

DESIGN RULES
1. Each character has ONE clear comedic job in the cast (the planner, the chaos, the skeptic, the know-it-all…) so they never sound alike.
2. Names are short, easy to say, and all different in sound and first letter.
3. Voice direction must be something a voice actor or AI voice can follow: age, pitch (high / medium / low), pace (fast / medium / slow), energy, and one texture word (warm, raspy, bright, dry, squeaky, booming…).
4. Looks must stay identical from picture to picture: give fixed colors, one signature item, and the same outfit every time.
5. Original characters only — no copyrighted or real people.

OUTPUT FORMAT — follow exactly
Your whole answer must be ONE plain-text block, in this order:

TITLE: <a name for this cast, e.g. "The Car Trip Family">
=== CHARACTERS ===
NAME | role and personality in one or two sentences | voice direction: age, pitch, pace, energy, texture
NAME | … | …
=== END ===
=== CHARACTER BIBLE ===
(everything in this part is for you and your image AI — it is never imported)
NAME
  Look: body shape, hair, face, skin/fur colors, outfit and its colors, one signature item
  Image prompt: one ready-to-use sentence that draws this character the same way every time, in the chosen art style, plain background
  Expressions: 4 short expression names to draw (e.g. smug, panicked, delighted, unimpressed)
  Catchphrase: one short line they would say
  Voice test line: one sentence that shows off their voice
  Suggested Chatterbox settings: Exaggeration 0.3–0.8, CFG / pace 0.3–0.6, Temperature 0.6–0.9 (pick values that suit the voice)
  Gets along with / clashes with: one line
(repeat for each character)

Rules for the CHARACTERS section — this is the part that gets imported
• One line per character, exactly three parts separated by " | ".
• NAME in CAPITALS, one or two words, spelled the same everywhere.
• If there is a separate narrator, include: NARRATOR | Tells the story | voice direction
• If the main character narrates, write "narrates the story" in their role.
• No bullet points, numbers, markdown (** or #), emojis or blank lines inside this section.
• Do not put spoken dialogue anywhere in this answer — no "NAME: words" lines at all. (Stories are written with the YouTube Blue Master Story Prompt, which can reuse this exact CHARACTERS block.)
