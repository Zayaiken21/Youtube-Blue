      Voice track

      Pick a language and press the button. Translating is ours and instant: it happens right here, on this device in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Speaking uses your Chatterbox server, because it's what clones your voices, so every character keeps their voice in every language. Panels and captions are timed to the real speech.

      The voice step needs your Chatterbox server running in Colab. Other languages use its Multilingual model; you'll be asked before it switches. Clips are saved on this device, so only changed panels are made again.

      Template
      Your own images are fitted to the video shape automatically, keeping their proportions and style.

      💬Subtitles

      Every panel has its own caption, so a subtitle never runs across two panels. They're placed automatically where they read best for the video shape. Edit any caption under Order & timing.

      Fill captions from a story

      Spoken lines from Story Studio are shared out across the panels in order (scene headings and [tags] are left out).

      Translation limit

      On a computer with Chrome or Edge, translation runs on the device: instant, private and unlimited (the first time a language is used, the browser downloads it once). Elsewhere, translations come from the free MyMemory service: about 5,000 characters a day per device, roughly 3 languages for a 2-minute story. Add an email address to raise that to about 50,000 a day. It's only sent to MyMemory, with the text being translated. Translations are saved, so each caption is only translated once.
