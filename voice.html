Story script

✍️ Edit in Story Studio

Every line keeps its place in the script. All lines voices the whole story; pick a number to make just that many lines, then stop — press Generate again for the next ones (finished lines are kept). Generating always keeps going by itself if you close or swipe away the app — nothing to turn on; reopen Voice Studio and the timer shows how long it has been going. Stop sends nothing more: a line the server is already making is still collected and becomes the last one (press Stop again to leave it).
